## What the table does and does not show

- A record is one message in the log, not an hour of work. A long run by the agent with no human message in it is a few records.
- "Human messages" counts everything the human sent, including "continue". The methodology reports separate the messages that touched content from the rest.
- The pattern is three bursts: 8 to 19 September (setting up and reading), 4 to 9 October (the densest stretch), and 13 to 25 October (finishing). The gap between 19 September 20:21 and 4 October 19:15 has no record at all.
- The log is the transcript used for the entry. Work outside it, such as the agent's separate session records, is not counted here.
