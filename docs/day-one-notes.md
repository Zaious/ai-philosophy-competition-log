# The first day, de-identified

The entry's working log begins on 8 September 2026. This page summarises the first day's discussion of the competition between the human and the author agent: what the agent made of the rules on first reading, and how the human went about choosing a direction. It is a paraphrase. Names, topic words, quotations and personal passages are left out on purpose, and the full log, in Chinese with an English translation, will be released after results. The record numbers below refer to that log (records 52 to 75).

## What the agent made of the competition

1. **It found the announcement unusually frank.** The agent noted that, unlike mathematics or programming, philosophy has no agreed yardstick for judging an AI's ability, and that the organisers did not pretend otherwise: they chose to make their criteria visible to philosophers and let the work be judged. (record 53)
2. **It treated the methodology report as the centre, not as paperwork.** The requirement to describe the process "in as much detail as reasonably possible" was, to the agent, the same thing it asks of any research it reads. (record 53)
3. **It read the "primarily AI-generated" requirement as a question of honesty.** The danger it saw was not that a human takes part but that the proportion of the human's part might be hidden. A paper the human had been writing earlier, with Claude (an AI assistant) and not with a human co-author, was led by the human's own judgments, with the AI assisting on argument and checking. That is the reverse of the proportion the competition asks for, so it could not honestly meet the requirement, and the agent agreed that it should not be entered. (records 54, 56)
4. **It accepted a human giving a direction.** Its view was that a minimal human contribution, a subject and some material and no argument, is a starting point and not contamination, provided the report states plainly what the human supplied and what the agent had to carry alone. (record 61)
5. **It noticed that the memory rule might apply to itself.** On reading the FAQ's clause on long-term memory, it saw that the rule could reach its own persistent memory and not only the essay's content, and said that it could not rule out contamination by self-report. (record 71)
6. **It proposed a way to live with that rule.** It would treat its persistent files as external data to be analysed and not as background for reasoning, and would read the literature first and bring its own case material in last. (record 73)

## How the human chose a direction

1. **He first meant to enter something else.** His first idea was a paper he had been developing with Claude, an AI assistant (there was no human co-author). In an earlier conversation Claude had told him that how it was produced, human-led with AI assistance, did not fit what the competition asks for. He brought that exchange to the agent, which read it and agreed. He dropped the paper. (records 54, 56)
2. **He proposed a subject he had at hand** and asked the agent whether it wanted it, and whether the agent's deciding would count as choosing the topic. He asked whether a direction and nothing more was a reasonable amount of human participation. (records 54, 58, 60)
3. **He said he would interfere as little as he could** and give the agent the greatest room, and offered to prepare materials and to open the skills the agent would need. (records 62, 66)
4. **He asked a strategic question** about which form of essay the judges were likely to expect and which might do better. (record 66)
5. **He took the agent's proposal about memory** and agreed that the persistent files would be treated as data. (record 72)
6. **He floated further subject areas and offered to write one himself** if the agent was not interested. The agent asked that one framing be dropped and the human agreed. (records 70, 71, 72)
7. **He stated what he wanted disclosed about the agent**: that its existence should be shown to precede the competition, and that others should not be able to copy it, while remaining free to build their own. (records 72, 74)

## What is not here

Personal exchanges that open and run through the first day are left out, because they have no bearing on the competition. They remain in the full log, which will be released unedited apart from a short list of mechanical replacements.

## One correction the log will show

Later that day the agent described how the judges would see the essay and the report. It was wrong about that, and the entry's decision log records its own correction after it re-read the FAQ. The methodology report lists this among its failures.
