# docs

Everything written about the entry, in three groups. Start with the release plan if you want to know what is public and what is not.

**[release-plan.md](release-plan.md)**: what is public now, what comes after results, what is not promised.

## about-the-entry/

The entry itself, described without its content.

- [human-involvement-checklist.md](about-the-entry/human-involvement-checklist.md): how much the human did and did not do, item by item against the organisers' lists of permitted and forbidden involvement.
- [models-and-workflow.md](about-the-entry/models-and-workflow.md): which models, with what is and is not measured, and the workflow in outline.
- [weekly-rhythm.md](about-the-entry/weekly-rhythm.md): the seven weeks of work, with when it happened and what kind of work it was.
- [the-author-agent.md](about-the-entry/the-author-agent.md): the author agent described in outline; founding documents and rule text after results.
- [custodian-workflow.md](about-the-entry/custodian-workflow.md): how the custodian has worked with AI on academic writing until now, what this entry changes, and the tools the agent used.
- [day-one-notes.md](about-the-entry/day-one-notes.md): the first day's discussion of the competition, de-identified.

## the-competition/

The competition, from its own pages and from public discussion.

- [official-sources.md](the-competition/official-sources.md): the organisers' pages and the facts they state, kept apart from our reading of them.
- [rules-map.md](the-competition/rules-map.md): the rules and FAQ mapped, including the rules that are easy to miss and the questions the pages leave open.
- [public-discussion.md](the-competition/public-discussion.md): what has been said in public, including the organisers' statements beyond the FAQ, with links.
- [related-entries.md](the-competition/related-entries.md): other public repositories from entrants, described neutrally.
- [timeline.md](the-competition/timeline.md): dates.

## for-entrants/

For anyone preparing their own entry.

- [methodology-report-guide.md](for-entrants/methodology-report-guide.md): a checklist from one entrant, including a way to lose your own session records.
- [verifying-ai-authorship.md](for-entrants/verifying-ai-authorship.md): what logs and commitments can and cannot prove about who had the ideas.
- The tool is in [tools/chainlog](../tools/chainlog).
