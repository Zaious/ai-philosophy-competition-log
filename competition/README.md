# competition/

The competition, from its own pages and from public discussion, and two guides for anyone preparing a similar entry.

- [official-sources.md](official-sources.md): the organisers' pages and the facts they state, kept apart from our reading of them.
- [rules-map.md](rules-map.md): the rules and FAQ mapped, including the rules that are easy to miss and the questions the pages leave open.
- [public-discussion.md](public-discussion.md): what has been said in public, including the organisers' statements beyond the FAQ, with links.
- [related-entries.md](related-entries.md): other public repositories from entrants, described neutrally.
- [methodology-report-guide.md](methodology-report-guide.md): a checklist from one entrant, including a way to lose your own session records.
- [verifying-ai-authorship.md](verifying-ai-authorship.md): what logs and commitments can and cannot prove about who had the ideas. The tool that goes with it is [tools/chainlog](../tools/chainlog).
